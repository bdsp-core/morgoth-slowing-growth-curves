Regional strip = per-region delta-excess deviation z (focal = one region high; diffuse = broadly elevated).

**Case 1 — Focal slowing · peak 5.1 SD (>99.9th centile) · prominent in W · 9M**  
- LENS (brief): Abundant right posterior theta–delta (mixed) slowing (max T4), present in wakefulness and sleep.  
- Report impression: focal slowing in the right posterior region  
- LENS (full): Right posterior theta–delta (mixed) slowing. Peak deviation 5.1 SD above the age- and stage-matched normal at that region (>99.9th centile), abnormal in 65% of analysed segments (abundant); longest continuous run ≈4.9 min over 15 episodes. Present in wakefulness and sleep.  
- Report description: on the right, there is near continuous 3-5 hz of focal slowing in the posterior quadrant…drowsiness was characterized by generalized background slowing and gradual dropout of the posterior dominant rhythm. stage ii sleep was seen, characterized by sleep spindles.  
- Report (structured): focal slowing, right posterior

Present only during sleep; most prominent in REM sleep.
